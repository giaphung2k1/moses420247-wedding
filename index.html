01 / Our story

One right swipe.
A forever kind
of love.

Our story started on a dating site, where one right swipe led us straight to forever. Rhonda’s big, beautiful eyes caught Monique’s attention, while Monique’s double dimples made it a little harder for Rhonda to keep playing it cool. Then came the promise ring, delivered in the most Rhonda way possible: “Here—and if you don’t like it, I kept the receipt.” The ring may have come with a return option, but Monique already knew there would be no returns—Rhonda was definitely a keeper. Since then, they’ve grown together in faith, found healing in one another, and built a love rooted in grace, laughter, and choosing each other every day.

02 / The beginning of forever

Two proposals.
One love story.

Why have one proposal when you can have two? Each woman had her own chance to ask the question.

A sunset. A gondola. A yes.

Monique’s proposal

A surprise date, a message in a bottle, and a question waiting beneath the lights in Fort Lauderdale.

Read the whole story +

Monique told Rhonda only one thing: she had planned a surprise date. The two took the train into Fort Lauderdale, stopped at a nearby hotel for a drink and a bite to eat, then stepped outside to find a private gondola waiting to carry them into the sunset.

Midway through the ride, after a playfully planned ‘bump’ in the water, they discovered a message in a bottle, filled with sentimental words straight from Monique’s heart. With the sun setting around them, the evening already felt perfect. The biggest surprise was still to come.

As the gondola returned to the dock, Rhonda spotted her daughter and granddaughter standing beside a saxophonist and an illuminated ‘Will You Marry Me?’ marquee. The sight of them brought her to tears.

Surrounded by music, love, and some of the people who mean the most to them, Monique led Rhonda beneath the lights, dropped to one knee, and asked her to spend forever together. Rhonda said yes, and for Monique, it was the most beautiful moment of her life.

Candlelight. Family. Forever.

Rhonda’s proposal

On Christmas Day, a path of rose petals led to a beautiful new memory filled with family, healing, and hope.

Read the whole story +

On Christmas Day, everyone in Rhonda’s family group chat knew what was about to happen—everyone except Monique and her mom. When they arrived at Rhonda’s house to celebrate, they stepped into complete darkness, illuminated only by a path of tea-light candles and rose petals. As ‘Why I Love You’ by MAJOR played softly in the background, Rhonda took Monique’s hand and led her through the candlelight.

At the end of the path was a glowing ‘Will You Marry Me?’ display. Surrounded by their family, Rhonda got down on one knee and asked Monique to become her wife. Monique said yes, and the room erupted into the warmest—and loudest—‘Awww!’

For Rhonda, the date represented so much more than their engagement. Eight years earlier, her mother went into cardiac arrest on Christmas night and passed away two weeks later. From that moment forward, Christmas carried the weight of an unimaginable loss.

Then, as Rhonda later wrote, ‘A little over 18 months ago, I met LOVE.’ Choosing Christmas Day for her proposal didn’t erase the painful memory—it made room beside it for a new one filled with family, healing, hope, and forever. Eight years after experiencing one of the most tragic days of her life, Rhonda experienced one of the most beautiful.
